Local processing

Your documents stay on your computer

There is no account, no cloud processing and no analytics. The one network step is the first-run download of the language models.

  • Translation, OCR, stamping and export all run locally
  • Models are checked against pinned SHA-256 hashes before use
  • Optional Secure Vault protects the app database with your password
  • Support logs hold codes and counts, never document text
