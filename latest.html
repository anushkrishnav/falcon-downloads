Check your download

Compare the file’s SHA-256 with the value shown above. On Windows, in PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\Falcon_*_x64-setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256. On Linux: sha256sum Falcon_*. If the values differ, do not install; contact support.
