Privacy & security

Falcon is built so your documents never need to leave your computer. Here is exactly what that means, and where it stops.

What stays on your computer

  • Translation, OCR, stamping, watermarking and export all run locally
  • No account, no cloud processing, no analytics or crash-report uploads
  • Logs hold codes, stages and counts only: never document text or file names

The one network step

During first-run setup Falcon downloads its language models from a public model host. That request is for Falcon’s own model files; no document or personal data is sent. Each file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 hash before it is used, and the models can also be installed offline from a folder. After setup Falcon translates with the network switched off.

Protection options

Standard

No password. Convenient, and protects against casual access only. The database key is stored in a protected file next to the database.

Secure Vault

Encrypts Falcon’s own database (job history, memory, terminology, settings) with your password and locks the app. Use it if the computer may be shared or lost.

What the vault does not do: it does not encrypt your documents or exports; they stay ordinary files in your workspace folder. For sensitive sets use full-disk encryption (BitLocker on Windows, LUKS on Linux).

Remove password protection

In Settings → Security, choose View all workspaces, select a workspace, then choose Unsecure…. Enter your current password and confirm Unsecure workspace. Falcon will open without a password on this device. Your documents and saved work are kept.

Protection is shared across workspace folders, so removing it applies to all of them. See the workspace protection instructions.

Built-in safeguards

  • Untrusted files are opened by separate helper programs, with size and structure checks for Office and PDF files
  • Archive extraction rejects unsafe paths; installers are hash-checked
  • The vault password has a minimum length and uses a memory-hard key derivation
  • Support bundles are redacted and refuse to save if a leak check fails

What Falcon does not claim

  • A stamp is an image mark, not a legal electronic signature
  • Machine translation can drop or change words; review it before relying on it, and use a certified translator where the law requires one
  • Falcon is not a compliance programme. The organisation using it remains responsible for its own legal duties under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law and any sector rules

The full written guide is available inside the app (Settings Privacy guide) as a PDF: Local Processing & Saudi PDPL Guide. It is informational and is not legal advice.
